
# My Favorite Movie

## Every Child Is Special 

**Genre:** Drama, Family

**Director:** Aamir Khan

### About the Movie

Every Child Is Special is an Indian movie about a young boy named Ishaan who struggles with his studies because he has dyslexia. His teachers and family misunderstand him until an art teacher recognizes his talent and helps him discover his abilities.

### Why I Like This Movie

- It reminds us that everyone has different talents and abilities.

### My Favorite Lessons

- We should never judge someone based on their weaknesses.
- Everyone deserves support and understanding.

**My Rating:** 10/10 ⭐
